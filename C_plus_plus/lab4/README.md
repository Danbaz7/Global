# Lab 4: Circle Area and Sphere Volume Calculator

This C++ program calculates the area of a circle and the volume of a sphere based on a user-provided radius. It demonstrates the use of functions and mathematical operations in C++.

## How It Works

- The user is prompted to enter a radius (a non-negative number).
- The program calculates:
  - The area of a circle using the formula:  
    `Area = π × radius²`
  - The volume of a sphere using the formula:  
    `Volume = (4/3) × π × radius³`
- The results are displayed to the user.
